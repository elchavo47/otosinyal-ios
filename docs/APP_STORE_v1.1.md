**Description:**
OTOSINYAL OBD2 turns your iPhone into a car diagnostic tool. Plug a compatible ELM327 OBD2 adapter into your car, connect over Bluetooth LE or Wi‑Fi, and see what your car's computer is reporting.

TROUBLE CODES
- Read stored, pending and permanent trouble codes (DTC)
- Plain-language descriptions for 4,193 generic codes plus brand-specific codes
- Clear codes and the check engine light, then verify the result with a re-scan

INSPECTION & USED CAR CHECK
- See if your car is ready for an emissions inspection: check engine light, trouble codes and readiness monitors
- Before buying a used car, find out whether trouble codes were cleared recently (distance and warm-up cycles since codes were cleared)
- Share the result as a PDF report

LIVE DATA
- Speed, RPM, coolant temperature, battery voltage, boost, MAF, fuel rail pressure and more
- Graphs and recording with CSV export
- Performance timer: 0-100, 60-100, 80-120 km/h with GPS comparison

VEHICLE HEALTH
- Vehicle health score from trouble codes, readiness and live data
- Freeze frame data and on-board test results (Mode 06)
- VIN detection and ECU identification

FOR VAG VEHICLES (VW, Audi, Skoda, Seat, Cupra)
- Advanced diagnostics and service functions
- Long coding and adaptations with automatic backup and restore. The Coding tab appears only when a compatible vehicle and adapter are connected.

MAINTENANCE
- Maintenance reminders with notifications
- Trip log and diagnostic history

ADAPTER REQUIREMENTS
iPhone works with Bluetooth LE (BLE) and Wi‑Fi ELM327 adapters. Bluetooth Classic adapters that pair with a PIN code in the phone's Bluetooth settings do not work on iPhone. The in-app "Which adapters work?" guide helps you choose.

No adapter yet? Try demo mode from the home screen.

All data stays on your device. OTOSINYAL does not require an account.

## App Review notu (App Review Information > Notes)

The app requires an ELM327 OBD2 adapter connected to a vehicle. To review without hardware, tap "No adapter? Try demo mode" (or the DEMO button) on the home screen; all screens then work with simulated vehicle data, including Live data, DTC and Tools > Inspection & Used Car Check.

The app's language follows the device language (Turkish devices: Turkish, all other languages: English) and can be changed in Settings > Language.

The Coding tab is intentionally shown only when a real VAG vehicle (VW, Audi, Skoda, Seat, Cupra) is connected over a CAN protocol with an adapter that passes the long-message (ISO-TP) test, because coding writes to the vehicle's control units. It is not available in demo mode. Write operations are also blocked while the vehicle is moving and always create a backup first.

Notification permission is only requested when the user marks a maintenance item as done, to remind them of the next due date.
